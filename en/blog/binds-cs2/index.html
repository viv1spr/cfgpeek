CS2 binds 2026: useful binds and the ones that no longer work

Binds that work in CS2 right now: grenades on their own keys, jump on the mouse wheel, quick switch and buy binds. Plus why you don't need a jumpthrow bind anymore.

How to set a bind

A bind is the command bind "key" "action". Type it into the console or put it in your autoexec to keep everything in one place. Every line below has a copy button.

Grenades on their own keys

The most useful bind: no more scrolling through grenades with one key. Each command just selects a slot, so these binds are allowed.

Grenades
bind "z" "slot7"   // flashbang
bind "x" "slot8"   // smoke
bind "c" "slot6"   // HE
bind "v" "slot10"  // molotov / incendiary

Jump on the mouse wheel

The wheel gives you more chances to jump at the right moment, which helps with tricky jumps. Space still works.

Jump on the mouse wheel
bind "mwheelup" "+jump"
bind "mwheeldown" "+jump"
bind "space" "+jump"

Quick switch, voice and buy binds

Quick switch and voice
bind "q" "lastinv"
bind "mouse4" "+voicerecord"

Buy binds still work: buying doesn't count as movement or attack.

Buy binds
bind "F1" "buy ak47; buy m4a1"
bind "F2" "buy vesthelm"
bind "F3" "buy smokegrenade; buy flashbang; buy hegrenade; buy molotov; buy incgrenade"
bind "F4" "buy defuser"

Binds that no longer work

Since 19 August 2024 CS2 ignores binds that combine several movement or attack inputs on one key. That covers jumpthrow (jump plus throw), walkthrow and binds that press left and right at once. On Valve servers such scripts can get you kicked.

Manual jumpthrows are easier now: the timing window is wider than in CS:GO, and the grenade preview shows the jumpthrow path while you hold jump.

FAQ

Does the jumpthrow bind work in CS2?

No. Since August 2024 binds that combine jump and throw are ignored, and Valve servers can kick you for them. Jumpthrows are done by hand.

How do I bind grenades in CS2?

With bind commands: slot6 is HE, slot7 flashbang, slot8 smoke and slot10 molotov or incendiary.
