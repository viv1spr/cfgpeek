What sensitivity CS2 pros use in 2026

The median eDPI of pros in our database is 800, and 60% play on 400 DPI. We break down pro sensitivity, show the fastest and slowest players and give you a calculator.

eDPI is mouse DPI multiplied by in-game sensitivity. It lets you compare players with different mice: 400 DPI at sens 2 and 800 DPI at sens 1 both give 800 eDPI.

Why pros play low sens

  • Low sens gives precise small movements: the crosshair doesn't jitter when you aim at a head.
  • Pros make big turns with the whole arm on a wide mousepad.
  • If you're used to high sens, lower it gradually, 10–15% at a time.

FAQ

What sensitivity do CS2 pros use?

The median eDPI of pros in our database is 800: for example 400 DPI at sens 2 or 800 DPI at sens 1.

How do I calculate eDPI?

Multiply your mouse DPI by your in-game sensitivity: 800 × 1.25 = 1000 eDPI.
