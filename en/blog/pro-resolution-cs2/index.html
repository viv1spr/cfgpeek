The most popular resolution is 1280×960: 63 of 92 players use it. 52 play 4:3 with black bars, 24 play 4:3 stretched and only 10 use 16:9.

Stretched or black bars

  • Stretched: the 4:3 image fills the whole wide monitor, so player models look wider.
  • Black bars: proportions stay correct, bars remain on the sides, and the field of view is the same as stretched.
  • 16:9: a wider view to the sides, but smaller models.

How to set stretched resolution

  1. In CS2 open Settings → Video and pick the 4:3 aspect ratio and 1280×960.
  2. To stretch it, enable full-screen scaling in your GPU control panel.

FAQ

What resolution do pros use in CS2?

Most pros play 1280×960 in 4:3, more often with black bars than stretched.

Why do pros play 4:3?

It's a CS:GO habit, and stretched makes enemy models bigger; black bars keep the proportions.
