How to pick an AWP crosshair

  • Start with an AWPer's crosshair from the list above and play a couple of maps.
  • If it hides heads at long range, cut the length by 1 pixel.
  • If you lose it on light walls, turn on the outline.

FAQ

What crosshair do AWPers use in CS2?

Usually a small cross around 2 pixels long. The averages for AWPers in our database are in the table above.

Do you need a separate crosshair for the AWP?

CS2 uses one crosshair for every weapon, so AWPers pick one that also works with rifles.
