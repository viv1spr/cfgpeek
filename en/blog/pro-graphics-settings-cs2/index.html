Why high shadows but low textures

Shadows show an enemy before they come round a corner, so pros keep them on. Textures, shaders and particles barely affect whether you can see a player but load the GPU, so they go to minimum.

Most use 4x or 8x MSAA: model edges don't shimmer and enemies are easier to spot at range.

FAQ

What graphics settings do CS2 pros use?

Most often: high shadows, low textures, shaders and particles, 4x or 8x MSAA, V-Sync off, fullscreen.

Should I cap FPS in CS2?

Pros differ: some use fps_max 0 with no cap, others lock it at 400–600 for steadier frame times.
