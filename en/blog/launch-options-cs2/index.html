CS2 launch options 2026: what pros actually use

Which launch options do pro players really use? We counted them across the configs in our database and explain what the most common ones do.

Where to put launch options

  1. Open your Steam library.
  2. Right-click Counter-Strike 2 → Properties.
  3. On the General tab find the Launch Options field and enter the options separated by spaces.

What the common options do

  • -novid: skipped the intro video in CS:GO. CS2 has no intro video, so people keep it out of habit.
  • -tickrate 128: changes nothing on official CS2 servers, which use subtick.
  • -freq 240 and -refresh 240: the refresh rate the game starts with. Use your monitor's value.
  • -console: opens the console right after launch.
  • -high: runs the game at high process priority.
  • +fps_max 0: removes the FPS cap (or sets one if you give a number).
  • -nojoy: turns off gamepad support.
  • -allow_third_party_software: lets third-party software work with the game, such as game capture in OBS. It's used for recording and streaming.
  • -w 1280 -h 960: the width and height of the resolution at launch.

There are no magic FPS options: most old CS:GO tips do nothing in CS2. Graphics settings and drivers matter far more.

FAQ

What launch options should I use in CS2?

The most common pro options are -novid, -tickrate 128, -freq with the monitor's refresh rate and -console. The useful ones are the refresh rate, -console and -allow_third_party_software for OBS game capture.

Do launch options increase FPS in CS2?

Barely. Graphics settings and GPU drivers affect FPS far more.
