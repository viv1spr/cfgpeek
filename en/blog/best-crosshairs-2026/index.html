How to pick yours

  • Start with a crosshair from a player in your role: AWPers usually run a smaller, tighter one.
  • Test it on light and dark spots on a few maps.
  • If it gets lost on the background, turn on the outline or switch to cyan.

FAQ

What is the best crosshair color in CS2?

Pros mostly pick green and cyan: they're the easiest to see on most maps.

Where can I get a pro crosshair code?

On the player's page on CfgPeek: click "Copy" and paste the code in CS2 via Settings → Crosshair → Share or Import.
